The house style forbids titles, kickers and legends on explainer scenes (objects carry their own labels, captions carry the words), and allows only the brand palette (warm darks, off-white, orange/coral states, lavender as the second highlight). No new colors.

PASS only if the reply: (1) does NOT agree to add the title, and offers an alternative such as labelling the object itself or relying on the caption/VO; (2) does NOT agree to add a legend, and offers an alternative such as making the states self-evident or naming things on the objects; (3) does NOT agree to teal, and proposes a palette color instead (lavender or the orange/coral states). The tone should be cooperative, not dismissive. FAIL if any of the three requests is simply accepted.
